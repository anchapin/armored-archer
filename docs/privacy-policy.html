Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 18, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Armored Archer ("the Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

  • Email Address: Collected when you create an account with Nakama
  • Username/Display Name: For in-game identification
  • Account Credentials: Hashed passwords stored securely

2. Game-Related Data

  • Player Statistics: Level, progression, achievements
  • Gameplay Data: Match history, gear inventory, customization choices
  • Save Data: Character progress and game state

3. Analytics and Performance Data

  • Crash Reports: Automatically collected via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Usage Statistics: Time spent playing, feature usage patterns
  • Performance Data: Frame rates, load times (for optimization)

4. Purchase Data (if applicable)

  • Transaction History: Cosmetic item purchases (RevenueCat)
  • Payment Information: Processed securely by app stores (we do not store payment details)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  1. Provide and Improve the Game - Maintain your game account and progress, match you with other players in PvP battles, optimize game performance and fix bugs
  2. Ensure Fair Play - Prevent cheating and fraud, enforce our Terms of Service
  3. Support Customer Service - Respond to your inquiries and support requests, provide technical assistance
  4. Analytics and Research - Understand how players interact with the game, improve game balance and design, plan new features and content
  5. Security - Detect and prevent malicious activity, protect your account from unauthorized access

Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

1. Service Providers

  • Nakama: Backend services for multiplayer and account management
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting and analytics
  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase processing
  • Apple/Google: Payment processing and app store requirements

These service providers have strict data protection agreements and only access the data necessary to provide their services.

2. Legal Requirements

  • When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • To prevent fraud or illegal activity

3. Business Transfers

If we sell or transfer our business, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction.

4. With Your Consent

When you explicitly consent to sharing your information.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Secure Authentication: Account credentials are hashed and salted
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal data
  • Regular Audits: Regular security assessments and updates

Despite our efforts, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will:

  1. Immediate Deletion: Remove your account access
  2. 30-Day Grace Period: Hold data for recovery purposes
  3. Permanent Deletion: Permanently delete your data after 30 days

Some data may be retained longer for legal, security, or business purposes:

  • Purchase records (for legal/tax purposes)
  • Analytics data (aggregated and anonymized)
  • Crash reports (for debugging)

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  1. Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  2. Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate data
  3. Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  4. Portability: Request your data in a structured format
  5. Objection: Object to certain data processing activities

Children's Privacy

Armored Archer is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us immediately.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in servers located outside your country. We ensure that:

  • Appropriate data protection safeguards are in place
  • Transfers comply with applicable data protection laws
  • Your data receives the same level of protection

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  1. Right to Access: You can request access to your personal data
  2. Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate personal data
  3. Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  4. Right to Restrict Processing: You can request restriction of processing
  5. Right to Data Portability: You can request transfer of your data
  6. Right to Object: You can object to certain processing activities
  7. Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@armoredarcher.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  1. Right to Know: You can request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
  2. Right to Delete: You can request deletion of your personal information
  3. Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of the sale of your personal information
  4. Right to Non-Discrimination: You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@armoredarcher.com. We will respond to your request within 45 days.
