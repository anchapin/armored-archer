## Purpose

This playbook equips the support team to help closed-beta users resolve common issues quickly and escalate when needed. It consolidates answers to frequent questions, troubleshooting steps for known problem areas, and the escalation path for issues beyond first-contact resolution.

## FAQ

### Getting Started

**Q: How do I join the closed beta?**
A: You need a beta invitation code (`BETA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`). Enter it in Settings > Account > Redeem Beta Access. If you received an email invitation, follow the link to generate your code.

**Q: Can I play with friends who aren't in the beta?**
A: No. Beta is a separate environment. You can only matchmake with other beta testers. Non-beta friends can join the waitlist.

**Q: Will my beta progress carry over to the live game?**
A: Yes. All progress, gear, and achievements earned during beta will transfer to the live version.

**Q: What platforms are supported?**
A: iOS (minimum iOS 15) and Android (minimum Android 10).

---

### Account & Login

**Q: I can't log in. What should I do?**
A: Try these steps in order:
1. Check your internet connection (WiFi or cellular).
2. Force-close the app and relaunch.
3. Clear session data: Settings > Account > Reset Session.
4. If using a VPN, try disabling it.
5. Check status.armoredarcher.com for server outages.
6. If still stuck, contact Discord `#beta-support` or email beta-support@armoredarcher.com.

**Q: My session expired and I can't reconnect.**
A: Session tokens expire after 24 hours. Force-close the app, relaunch, and log in again. If this fails repeatedly, clear session data (Settings > Account > Reset Session).

**Q: I forgot which account I used for beta.**
A: Email beta-support@armoredarcher.com with the device you registered on and approximate signup date. We'll look it up.

---

### Connectivity

**Q: I keep getting "No internet connection" but my WiFi works.**
A: The game servers may be down or under maintenance. Check status.armoredarcher.com. If servers are up, try switching between WiFi and cellular, then relaunch.

**Q: The game lags or stutters during combat.**
A: Try these steps:
1. Close other apps to free memory.
2. Lower graphics quality in Settings > Display.
3. Ensure you're on a stable connection (WiFi preferred).
4. Restart the app if lag persists after 2+ minutes.
5. Report the issue via in-game feedback so we can investigate server-side.

**Q: I got disconnected mid-match. Will I lose progress?**
A: Combat progress is saved server-side. If you reconnect within 5 minutes, the session resumes. Otherwise, the match result is recorded as a loss. XP earned before disconnect is preserved.

### Gameplay

**Q: How does auto-aim work?**
A: Auto-aim targets the nearest enemy within range. You can adjust aim sensitivity in Settings > Controls. Hold the fire button to auto-target, or tap specific enemies to prioritize them.

**Q: My arrows pass through enemies.**
A: This is a known latency issue under poor connections. Try the connectivity troubleshooting above. If it happens consistently on a strong connection, submit a bug report with your device model and OS version.

**Q: How does gear rarity work?**
A: Gear comes in four rarities: Common (gray), Rare (blue), Epic (purple), Legendary (gold). Higher rarity gear has better base stats. You can upgrade gear using crafting materials from campaign stages.

**Q: How do I equip gear?**
A: Open your Inventory from the main menu, select a gear piece, and tap Equip. You have 5 slots: helm, armor, bow, arrow, amulet. Your loadout is saved automatically.

**Q: What are gems and how do I socket them?**
A: Gems are enhancements found in campaign stages. Open Inventory > select a gear piece > tap an empty socket > choose a gem. Each gem type provides a different stat bonus.

### PvP & Matchmaking

**Q: How does async PvP work?**
A: Asynchronous PvP lets you battle other players without needing to be online at the same time. You set your defense formation, and attackers fight your formation controlled by AI. You attack others' formations the same way.

**Q: Matchmaking takes too long.**
A: Beta has a smaller player pool. Try queuing during peak hours (6-10 PM local time). If queues exceed 3 minutes, cancel and requeue. We're monitoring queue times and adjusting brackets.

**Q: I think I fought a hacker / someone with impossible stats.**
A: Report via in-game feedback with the player's name and what seemed off. All combat is calculated server-side, so cheating is extremely unlikely, but we investigate every report.

---

### Progression

**Q: I stopped gaining XP.**
A: You may have hit the current beta level cap. Check the announcements channel in Discord for the current cap. XP earned above the cap is stored and applied when the cap increases.

**Q: How do ability points work?**
A: You earn ability points each level. Spend them in the Skills menu to boost stats (damage, speed, health, crit rate). Points can be reset for free during beta.

**Q: Seasonal leaderboards seem empty.**
A: Leaderboards populate as more players join the season. Seasons reset periodically during beta to test the system. Your seasonal rewards are tracked even if the leaderboard resets.

---

### Store & Purchases

**Q: Can I make real purchases during beta?**
A: Beta uses a sandbox payment environment. No real charges occur. All purchases are free during beta. Your beta purchases will not carry over to live; you'll receive equivalent value at launch.

**Q: The store shows an error.**
A: Store errors usually mean a temporary server issue. Wait a few minutes and try again. If persistent, report via in-game feedback.

---

### Technical Issues

**Q: The game crashes on launch.**
A: Try these steps:
1. Restart your device.
2. Ensure you have at least 1 GB free storage.
3. Update to the latest beta build (check Discord for build announcements).
4. Uninstall and reinstall (progress is stored server-side).
5. If still crashing, email beta-support@armoredarcher.com with your device model and OS version.

**Q: The game drains my battery quickly.**
A: Lower graphics quality in Settings > Display. Close background apps. On iOS, enable Low Power Mode. On Android, enable Battery Saver. We're optimizing battery usage throughout beta.

**Q: Notifications aren't working.**
A: Check that notifications are enabled in your device settings for Armored Archer. On iOS: Settings > Notifications > Armored Archer. On Android: Settings > Apps > Armored Archer > Notifications.

**Q: The app is taking up too much storage.**
A: The game caches assets locally for faster loading. You can clear the cache in Settings > Storage > Clear Cache. This won't affect your progress.

---

### Feedback & Reporting

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Three options:
1. **In-game** (preferred): Settings > Report Bug. Auto-attaches device info and logs.
2. **Discord**: Use the `/bug-report` command in `#beta-bug-reports`.
3. **Email**: beta-support@armoredarcher.com for sensitive issues.

**Q: How do I suggest a feature?**
A: Submit via in-game feedback (Settings > Send Feedback) and select "Suggestion" as the category. You can also post in Discord `#beta-suggestions`.

**Q: How do I check the status of my bug report?**
A: Check GitHub Issues with the `beta` label, or ask in Discord `#beta-support` with your report number.

## Troubleshooting Guides

### Connection Issues (Detailed)

Follow this decision tree for any connection-related report:

```
User reports connection issue
  |
  +-- Can they open the app at all?
  |     |
  |     +-- NO --> Crash on launch --> See "Crash on Launch" above
  |     |
  |     +-- YES --> Can they reach the main menu?
  |           |
  |           +-- NO --> Stuck on loading screen
  |           |     --> Check status page, clear cache, reinstall
  |           |
  |           +-- YES --> Error message?
  |                 |
  |                 +-- "No internet" --> Check WiFi/cellular, check status page
  |                 +-- "Session expired" --> Reset session in Settings
  |                 +-- "Server maintenance" --> Check Discord/status page for schedule
  |                 +-- "Authentication failed" --> Reset session, reinstall if persistent
  |                 +-- Timeout --> Check connection stability, try different network
```

### Performance Issues (Detailed)

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Low FPS (< 30) | Device thermal throttle | Close other apps, lower graphics, let device cool |
| Stuttering | Memory pressure | Close background apps, clear cache |
| Long load times | Slow network or old device | Use WiFi, ensure latest build |
| Audio crackling | CPU overload | Lower graphics, close other apps |
| Touch input lag | Display scaling issue | Set graphics to Medium or Low |

## Escalation Path

### When to Escalate

Escalate any issue that cannot be resolved with the troubleshooting steps above, or that matches these criteria:
- Affects multiple users (2+ reports of the same issue)
- Involves data loss or progress loss
- Involves potential security issues or exploits
- Requires a code fix or server-side change

### Escalation Levels

| Level | Role | Trigger | Response Time |
|-------|------|---------|---------------|
| L1 | Support Team (Discord `#beta-support`) | First contact, all issues | < 2 hours |
| L2 | On-Call Engineer | L1 cannot resolve in 30 min, multi-user impact | < 15 min from escalation |
| L3 | Technical Lead | Requires code fix, architectural decision | < 30 min from escalation |
| L4 | Development Lead / Executive | Data breach, extended outage, legal concern | < 1 hour from escalation |

### Escalation Process

1. **L1 to L2**: Support member posts in Discord `#beta-escalations` with a summary including: user, issue, steps tried, severity.
2. **L2 to L3**: On-call engineer creates a GitHub Issue with `severity:high` or `severity:critical` and notifies in `#engineering`.
3. **L3 to L4**: Technical lead emails the development lead with incident summary and recommended action.

### Severity Classification for Escalation

| Severity | Examples | SLA Target |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Critical | Crash on launch, data loss, progression wipe, security exploit | Resolve < 24 hours |
| High | Feature broken for all users, frequent disconnects, store errors | Resolve < 72 hours |
| Medium | Feature partially broken, workaround exists, visual glitches | Resolve < 1 week |
| Low | Cosmetic issues, minor inconvenience, suggestions | Next release |

## Incident Response Quick Reference

When a widespread issue is detected:

1. **Verify**: Confirm the issue affects multiple users (check Discord, GitHub, monitoring).
2. **Communicate**: Post in Discord `#beta-announcements` and update status page within 15 minutes.
3. **Triage**: Classify severity, assign to on-call engineer.
4. **Update**: Post updates per severity SLA:
   - Critical: Every 15 minutes
   - High: Every 30 minutes
   - Medium: Every 2 hours
5. **Resolve**: Deploy fix, verify, announce resolution.
6. **Review**: Post-incident summary within 24 hours.
