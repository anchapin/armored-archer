## Overview

Visual improvements milestone implementing the "Gilded Quest" design direction ("Tactile Heroism"). Focused equally on interface (UI) and gameplay visuals with a warm, editorial aesthetic featuring royal blue (#0060ce), gold, and emerald on warm parchment (#fdffda).

## Design System: Gilded Quest

**Creative North Star:** "The Digital Pop-Up Book" — tactile, high-energy heroics
**Vibe:** Warm, editorial, celebratory with intentional overlap and asymmetric depth

**Key Rules:**
- No 1px borders — separate with volume/layers
- No sharp corners — min 8px radius
- Use gradients for hero CTAs (15-degree angle)
- Fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans (display) + Be Vietnam Pro (body)
