# Plan: 01-03 Hit Particles + Death Explosions

**Phase:** 01 - Particle Effects Foundation
**Plan ID:** 01-03
**Objective:** Integrate hit and death particle effects with CombatManager

## Tasks

### Task 1: Connect CombatManager to EffectsManager
- **Action:** Modify CombatManager to emit signals on enemy hit/death:
  - Add signal: enemy_hit(position: Vector2, damage: int)
  - Add signal: enemy_death(position: Vector2, enemy_type: String)
  - Connect signals to EffectsManager methods
- **Verify:** Signals emit at correct times during combat
- **Done:** CombatManager signals connected to EffectsManager

### Task 2: Implement spawn_hit() in EffectsManager
- **Action:** Create hit effect spawning logic:
  - Get combat_hit particle scene from pool
  - Position at hit location
  - Configure one-shot emission
  - Auto-return to pool after lifetime
- **Verify:** Particles appear on enemy damage
- **Done:** Hit particles functional

### Task 3: Implement spawn_death() in EffectsManager
- **Action:** Create death effect spawning logic:
  - Get enemy_death particle scene from pool
  - Position at enemy location
  - Configure burst emission with more particles
  - Auto-return to pool after lifetime
- **Verify:** Particles appear on enemy defeat
- **Done:** Death explosions functional

### Task 4: Integrate arrow trail particles
- **Action:** Attach trail particles to Arrow scene:
  - Add arrow_trail particle as child of Arrow
  - Configure trail to follow arrow position
  - Enable one-shot if arrow destroys target
- **Verify:** Trail follows arrow during flight
- **Done:** Arrow trail functional

## Dependencies
- 01-01 (EffectsManager must exist)
- 01-02 (ParticlePool must exist)

## Wave
2 (depends on completion of 01-01 and 01-02)

## Success Criteria
- Hit particles spawn on every enemy hit
- Death explosions spawn on enemy defeat
- Arrow trails follow projectiles
- All effects return to pool after emission
