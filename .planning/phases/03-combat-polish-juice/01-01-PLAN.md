# Architecture Decisions
- CombatJuiceManager autoload as central coordinator for all juice effects
- ImpactManager handles screen shake and impact VFX
- DamageIndicatorManager handles floating damage numbers with color coding
- Player hit reactions in player_character.gd (flinch/stutter)
- Enemy death animations in base_enemy.gd (ragdoll physics)
- Signal-based integration between managers and game entities

# Design Decisions
- Screen shake intensity scales with attack strength (light, medium, heavy)
- Damage numbers use color coding: green (weak hits < 50%), yellow (normal 50-100%), red (critical > 100%)
- Hit reactions: brief flinch (200ms) or stutter (50ms pause) on damage taken
- Death animations: fade out + fall + particle burst for satisfying kills
</context>

<specifics>
# Impact Effects (JUICE-01)
- Light attacks: Small screen shake (2px, 0.2s duration), minor particle burst
- Medium attacks: Medium screen shake (5px, 0.4s duration), moderate particle burst
- Heavy attacks: Large screen shake (10px, 0.6s duration), large particle burst with camera zoom

# Damage Indicators (JUICE-02)
- Weak hits (< 50% of max): Green damage number, small scale, fade fast (0.5s)
- Normal hits (50-100%): Yellow damage number, normal scale, fade normal (1.0s)
- Critical hits (> 100%): Red damage number, large scale, slow fade (1.5s), slight bounce

# Hit Reactions (JUICE-03)
- Player flinch: Brief sprite shake + color flash white (200ms), return to normal
- Player stutter: Brief velocity pause (50ms), then resume
- Apply to all damage sources (melee, ranged, DoT)

# Death Animations (JUICE-04)
- Enemy death: Fade out to 0% opacity over 0.5s
- Ragdoll physics: Apply random rotation and velocity to death sprite
- Particle burst: Spawn 5-10 particles on death with spread effect
- Screen flash: Brief screen white-out (0.1s) for dramatic kills
</specifics>
