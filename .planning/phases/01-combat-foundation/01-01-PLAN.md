# From Research
Combat system needs unified timing approach:
- PvP and PvE share damage formulas but calculate differently (client-authoritative vs server-authoritative)
- Weapon types need distinct patterns: attack speed, range, projectile velocity curves
- Hit detection requires server-side validation for PvP to prevent cheating

# Architecture Decisions
- Use singleton CombatManager (autoload) for all combat calculations
- Damage formulas live in CombatManager, not distributed
- Hit detection uses raycast for projectiles, hitboxes for melee
- Weapon variety through inheritance or composition pattern (base Weapon class → Bow, Crossbow, etc.)
- PvP damage calculated on server, validated against client-reported hits

# Design Decisions
- Attack timing: Fixed frames based on weapon type and attack state (wind-up: X frames, attack: Y frames, release: Z frames)
- Hit feedback: Combination of floating damage numbers, screen shake, and hit stop frames
- Damage display: Color-coded (green/yellow/red) floating numbers with fade-out effect
</context>

<specifics>
# Attack Timing System
- Fast weapons: 12-frame wind-up, 6-frame attack, 3-frame release
- Medium weapons: 18-frame wind-up, 9-frame attack, 5-frame release
- Heavy weapons: 24-frame wind-up, 12-frame attack, 7-frame release
- Crossbows: Additional 4 frames for reload action

# Damage Formula Structure
- Base damage = weapon.attack_stat * (player.attack_stat / 100)
- Multipliers: headshot (2.0x), bodyshot (1.5x), legs (0.8x)
- Armor reduction: calculated before damage is applied
- PvP validation: server sends final damage value, client validates and applies

# Hit Detection Patterns
- Projectiles: Raycast on spawn, sphere collision at impact, destroy on timeout
- Melee attacks: Hitbox overlap test at damage frame, state-based hit (only register hit when in HIT state)
</specifics>
